My research is interdisciplinary, theoretical work centered on probability and optimization that touches information geometry, molecular computation, and submodular optimization. I work with Hong Qian and Jeff Bilmes. Earlier in my PhD I also worked on blockchain systems.

I'm in my final year and on the job market for quantitative, teaching, or postdoctoral roles.

I've also spent a good chunk of my time at UW as a teaching assistant. See my [teaching page](/teaching/) for the full list of courses, [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/axlevisu/) for my work experience, and [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=keXIydkAAAAJ&hl=en) for my publications.
